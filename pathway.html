Digital Accessibility

Digital Accessibility Pathway Program

Explore a structured pathway for building digital accessibility knowledge and skills.

Overview

Build accessibility into the way you create and deliver digital course materials. Explore practical approaches that help make digital learning experiences more usable and accessible for all students.

Discover Track

Learn core principles of digital accessibility, identify common barriers in course materials, and begin incorporating accessible design practices into your teaching. Workshop topics include:

  • What is Digital Accessibility?
  • Learn and Accessibility
  • Making Documents Accessible
  • Making Slide Presentations Accessible
  • Writing Great Alt Text
  • Accessible Media 101

Deep Dive Track

Apply accessibility practices across course materials and digital learning experiences, evaluate their effectiveness, and develop sustainable approaches for maintaining accessible courses. Both programs are offered on a quarterly basis.
  • Course Accessibility Accelerator: This six-week facilitated program helps instructors update course materials in advance of the quarter they are planning to teach.
  • Digital Accessibility for Course Design Intensive: This day and half format is designed to equip educators with the essential knowledge and skills to build accessible learning environments.
